WHAT ARE FRUITS?

Fruits are the seed-bearing structures in flowering plants that are formed from the ovary after flowering. They are typically sweet or sour and contain seeds.

Simple Fruits

Simple fruits develop from a single ovary of a single flower. They can be either fleshy or dry. Examples include apples, cherries, and tomatoes.
